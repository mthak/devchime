A few practical questions

Let’s get aligned.

When should we reach out?+

When you have a working product and some initial traction. That could mean engaged users, active pilots, repeat usage, or early revenue. Tell us what you’ve built, who uses it, and what you’ve learned.

How do you help beyond the investment?+

Our technical and sales leaders can work with you on product direction, technical decisions, positioning, go-to-market strategy, and customer acquisition. We start with the challenges where our experience is most relevant.

What should we include in an introduction?+

A brief introduction to the team, a product link or demo, your target customer, early traction, and the problem you want help solving next. A deck is welcome if you have one.
